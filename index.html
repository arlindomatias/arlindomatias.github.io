About

I am a pharmacologist working across the wet lab, computational analysis and clinical research. My main question is why many patients develop memory and attention problems during and after cancer treatment, a condition known as chemotherapy-related cognitive impairment, or chemobrain.

My postdoctoral project at FCFRP-USP, supervised by Prof. Sabrina F. de Sousa Lisboa, uses omics analyses to map the molecular changes behind chemobrain. It is part of an international collaboration with Aarhus University, Denmark.

Research

Chemobrain mechanisms. Transcriptomic and other omics analyses of brain tissue after chemotherapy, aiming to identify pathways that link treatment to cognitive decline.

Cannabinoids and cognition. My PhD tested cannabidiol (CBD) as a way to prevent cognitive deficits caused by chemotherapy in animal models.

Computational pharmacology. Reproducible pipelines for RNA-seq, genomics and phylogenetics, molecular docking, and machine learning for behavioural analysis.

Projects

  • bulk-rna-seq

    End-to-end bulk RNA-seq analysis of a public mouse chemotherapy dataset (GSE178812): differential expression, protein interaction networks and pathway enrichment, run as a single Make pipeline.

    R, DESeq2, STRINGdb, clusterProfiler, GNU Make

  • phylo-16s-pilot

    Phylogenetic trees built from public bacterial 16S barcoding sequences downloaded from NCBI GenBank.

    Snakemake, NCBI Entrez, multiple sequence alignment

  • Variant calling pipeline

    From raw reads (FASTQ) to variants (VCF), tested on the Genome in a Bottle HG002 sample, chromosome 20.

    Snakemake, Bash, conda

  • Seizure quantification in zebrafish in progress

    Markerless pose estimation to quantify seizure behaviour in adult zebrafish, validated against manual scoring.

    DeepLabCut, Python, PyTorch
